Research Directions

At the University at Buffalo (SUNY) in the Department of Biochemistry, Jacobs School of Medicine and Biomedical Sciences, The Lai Lab investigates how protein complexes read, remodel, and regulate the eukaryotic genome across dynamic cellular states and human disease. Supported by an NIH NIGMS R35 and national NSF ACCESS cyberinfrastructure, our research spans four complementary thrusts across experimental epigenomics, bioinformatic algorithms, generative biomolecular modeling, and open-science gateways.
