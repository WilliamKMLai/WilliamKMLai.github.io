Software & Science Gateways

The Lai Lab develops open-source algorithms, high-throughput computing containers, and turnkey web gateways built around FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable) principles. From base-pair chromatin footprinting and provenance verification to consistency-distilled biomolecular diffusion models, our tools are engineered to make large-scale computation accessible across both command-line HPC pipelines and graphical interfaces.
